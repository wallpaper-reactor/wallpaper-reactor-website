**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

This agreement applies every time you upload a wallpaper to Wallpaper Reactor, whether from the app, the command-line tool, or an agent acting on your account. It sits alongside our [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service/) and [Wallpaper Guidelines](/docs/wallpaper-creation/wallpaper-guidelines/). Where this agreement and the Terms of Service disagree about uploaded wallpapers, this agreement wins.

## The short version

- **You keep ownership.** Your wallpaper stays yours. We don't claim any ownership of it.
- **You can remove it at any time.** Unpublish or delete it in the app, and we stop offering it.
- **We may maintain it.** We may edit your wallpaper so it keeps working, for example for a new Godot version, a performance problem, a new device, or a security fix.
- **We may feature it.** We may use your wallpaper in advertising and marketing for Wallpaper Reactor.

## 3. We may maintain your wallpaper

You agree that we may modify your wallpaper, and publish the modified version in place of the earlier one, at any time and without asking first, to:

- keep it working with **new engine, runtime or platform versions** (for example, re-exporting a Godot wallpaper for a newer Godot release, or migrating a shader to a newer shader format);
- fix **performance, battery or heat** problems, including reducing resolution, frame rate or bitrate;
- **convert or re-encode** files (for example to a video format a platform requires) and generate previews;
- fix **security** problems or remove behaviour that breaks our guidelines;
- correct or translate its **title, description, tags and options**, and apply tags such as content or performance warnings.

When we do, we'll aim to keep the wallpaper's look, behaviour and intent as close to yours as the change allows. These edits are maintenance, not a claim of ownership: the modified wallpaper is still yours under section 1. If you don't like a change, you can tell us or remove the wallpaper.

To the extent the law allows, you agree not to assert any moral right (such as a right to object to modification) against these maintenance changes.

## 4. Removing your wallpaper

You can unpublish or delete your wallpaper at any time in the app. Deleting your account removes all of your wallpapers in the same way.

- **We stop offering it** to new users as soon as you remove it, and remove it from listings, search and our public file servers.
- **Copies users already downloaded** stay on their devices and keep working for them, under the licence in section 2. We don't reach into people's devices to delete them.
- **Our stored copies** are deleted, except that backups roll off on their normal schedule and we may keep a record (for example, that the wallpaper existed and who uploaded it) where we need it for safety, fraud prevention, a legal claim, or a moderation decision.
- **Advertising and marketing material** made before your removal may continue to be used; we won't create new material with it after you remove it.

## 5. Our right to refuse or take down

We review public wallpapers before they're published and may refuse, unpublish or remove any wallpaper at our discretion, including one that breaks our guidelines, the law, or someone else's rights, or that is unsafe or too demanding for devices to run. A wallpaper taken down for those reasons may also be removed from users' devices.

## 6. What you promise

When you upload a wallpaper, you confirm that:

- you created it, or you have **all the rights and licences needed** to upload it and grant the licence above — including for fonts, music, 3D models, images and code made by others, with any attribution they require included in the upload;
- it doesn't infringe anyone's rights or break the law;
- it contains **no malware** and doesn't try to access anything beyond what a wallpaper needs;
- it follows our [Wallpaper Guidelines](/docs/wallpaper-creation/wallpaper-guidelines/).

If you choose an open licence for your wallpaper in the app, that licence also applies to users alongside this agreement.

If you upload through an automated tool or an agent, you are responsible for what it uploads on your account as if you had uploaded it yourself.

## 7. Changes to this agreement

We may update this agreement. If a change materially affects the rights you give us, we'll tell you in the app before it applies to wallpapers you've already uploaded, and you can remove them if you don't agree.
