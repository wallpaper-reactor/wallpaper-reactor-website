## How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your **Wallpaper Reactor** account, either:

- in the app, open your account page and choose **Delete Account**, which starts the email below for you; or
- follow these steps:
  1. Email kyle@skylines.sh from the email address associated with your account
  2. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line
  3. Request deletion of your account in the email body

Your account deletion will be completed within a few days of receiving your request.

## What Data is Deleted vs. Kept

**Data that will be deleted:**
- Your sign-in, email address and profile (display name, avatar link, bio)
- Your favourites
- All of your wallpapers, public and private, and their files, as described in the [Creator Agreement](/creator-agreement/)
- Your Creator Agreement acceptance records
- Crash reports sent by app versions before 0.29 that carry your email address

**Data that will be kept:**
- Copies of your public wallpapers that other people already downloaded stay on their devices
- Feedback you sent from the app, which is not linked to your account
- A limited record where we need it for safety, fraud prevention, a legal claim or a moderation decision

Backups roll off on their normal schedule. Apart from those and the records above, once your account is deleted your personal data is permanently removed from our systems. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for everything we collect.
