**Wallpaper Engine is not free.** It's a paid app: at the time of writing, Steam sells it as a one-time purchase for US$4.99, and there is no official free version or trial. If you want live wallpapers without paying, Wallpaper Reactor is a free alternative.

The main Wallpaper Engine app runs on Windows only; a separate companion app exists for Android. Prices change and Steam runs sales, so check the [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/431960/Wallpaper_Engine/) for today's price in your region.

## Is there a free version of Wallpaper Engine?

No. The only official way to get it is to buy it on Steam. Sites that offer a "free download" of a paid app are unofficial copies, and those are a common way to pick up malware. If the price is the problem, a free app built for the job is the safer route.

## Wallpaper Reactor is free to use

Wallpaper Reactor creates, manages and applies animated wallpapers. These are free:

- **The app** on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android
- **The whole wallpaper catalogue.** Browse it, search it and apply anything in it, with no account.
- **Your own files.** Any video you already have can be [set as a wallpaper in seconds](/blog/set-any-video-as-live-wallpaper/).
- **Creating and uploading your own wallpapers** once you sign in with a free account. The [creation docs](/docs/wallpaper-creation/) cover shaders, video, web scenes and more.
- **Cloud favorites**, so your favorites follow you between devices
- **Battery and performance settings**

### What Pro adds

There is an optional paid plan, Pro. It adds private wallpapers, larger wallpaper files, settings sync and priority support, and it helps fund development. Nothing above needs it. The [features page](/features/) lists exactly what each tier includes.

## Getting started

1. **Download the app** from the [releases page](/releases/). Windows, macOS, Linux and Android builds are all there.
2. **Open Browse** and pick a wallpaper. No account is needed.
3. **Press Set.** The wallpaper starts running behind your icons.
