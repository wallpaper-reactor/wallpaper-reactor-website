# Wallpaper Engine for Mac

<p style="color: #a3a3a3; font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.7;">
Last updated: August 2026
</p>

Wallpaper Engine is Windows-only. There's no macOS version, no beta, and no announced plan for one — so if you're searching for it on a Mac, you need a replacement.

The problem with most replacements is that they're video players. They put a clip behind your icons and stop there. That's one slice of what Wallpaper Engine did, and it's why switching usually feels like losing something.

## Wallpaper Reactor is the only Mac app with truly interactive wallpapers

Web scenes take real input, shaders render live on your GPU, and full game scenes run on your desktop.

- **Interactive web wallpapers** that respond to your input
- **GLSL shaders** rendered in real time
- **Godot scenes** running as live wallpapers
- **Rive animations**
- **Video loops** in MP4 and WebM
- **Your own uploads** — build and publish your own wallpapers
- **Hundreds of wallpapers** in the gallery, growing

It's also battery-aware by default: auto-pauses under battery saver, with power-saving modes for when you're unplugged.

## One library, three platforms

Most Mac live wallpaper apps are Mac-only, which makes leaving a Windows-only app for one of them a lateral move.

Wallpaper Reactor runs on **macOS, Windows, and Android**:

- Set a wallpaper on your Mac, find it waiting on your PC
- Favorites sync when you're signed in; settings sync on the paid tier
- Per-display configs, so multi-monitor setups are handled properly
- Wallpapers you upload are available on every device you sign in on

If you've got a MacBook, a desktop, and a phone, Wallpaper Reactor treats them as one setup.
